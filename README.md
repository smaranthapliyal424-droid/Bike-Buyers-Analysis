# Bike Buyers Analysis

## Project Overview
An Excel-based data analysis project focused on understanding customer demographics and identifying patterns in bike purchasing behaviour. The project uses customer data to explore how factors such as age, income, gender, education, and commute distance relate to bike purchase decisions.

## Dataset
The dataset contains **1,000 customer records** with attributes including:

- Customer ID
- Marital Status and Gender
- Income and Number of Children
- Education and Occupation
- Home Ownership and Number of Cars
- Commute Distance
- Region, Age, and Age Bracket
- Purchased Bike (Yes/No)

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Data filtering and analysis

## Analysis Performed
- Analysed bike purchases across different age groups.
- Compared purchasing behaviour by gender and customer demographics.
- Examined the relationship between commute distance and bike purchases.
- Explored customer characteristics such as income, education, occupation, and home ownership.
- Used PivotTables and PivotCharts to summarise and visualise the findings.

## Key Findings
- Out of 1,000 customers, **481 purchased a bike**, while 519 did not.
- The Middle Age group recorded the highest number of bike purchases (383).
- Customers with a commute distance of 2–5 miles had the highest purchase rate among the commute-distance categories (approximately 58.6%).
- Customers commuting more than 10 miles recorded the lowest purchase rate (approximately 29.7%).

## Project Objective
The objective is to identify customer segments and purchasing patterns that can help businesses better understand their target audience and make data-informed marketing decisions.
